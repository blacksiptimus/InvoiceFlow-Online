InvoiceFlow

Invoices and quotes for freelancers. Published by Aqyrix Technologies for iPhone and Android.

InvoiceFlow is for people who bill their own clients. Create an invoice or a quote, add the line items, and share it as a PDF. Keep the client list, the numbering, and a record of what is paid and what is still outstanding.

Invoices and quotes

Each document has a client, line items, tax, discount, and a due date. You can preview the PDF, share it, save a template, and turn a quote into an invoice. Your own prefixes and next numbers stay in the app.

Clients

Save a name, email, phone, and address. Colour tags group the list. Open a client to see the documents you have already made for them.

On your phone

Invoices, clients, and the business profile stay on the phone. You can add a logo and choose the currency. The app runs in English, Arabic, and French. Arabic uses a right-to-left layout. Light and dark appearance are both available.

The free version includes up to 5 clients and up to 10 invoices per month. InvoiceFlow Pro is a one-time purchase. It removes those limits and includes PDF themes and backup and restore. Payment is handled by Apple or Google.

Backup

You can export a backup file, or sign in and upload one cloud copy. Restore asks before it replaces what is on the phone. An account is optional. Deleting the account removes the cloud copy and leaves the phone records in place.

Contact

Questions can be sent to developer@aqyrix.com. The privacy policy explains what the app does with information, including how to delete an account.
