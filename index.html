InvoiceFlow privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026

InvoiceFlow is published by Aqyrix Technologies. This policy explains what the InvoiceFlow app for iPhone and Android does with information. The app id is com.aqyrix.invoiceflow.pro. You create invoices and quotes, keep client records, and see what is still unpaid.

What stays on your phone

Your business profile, clients, invoices, quotes, templates, and settings stay on your phone. InvoiceFlow does not upload them unless you export a file, share a PDF, or upload a cloud backup.

Deleting a client, invoice, or quote removes that record from the phone. Deleting the app removes the records kept only on that phone.

Optional account

You can use InvoiceFlow without an account. If you create one, the account is an email address and a password. The password is stored as a protected hash. InvoiceFlow does not send marketing email. The app does not send a password-reset email, so a forgotten password cannot be recovered after you sign out.

The account is provided by Google Firebase. Firebase stores the email address and the account identifier. It does not receive your invoices unless you upload a backup.

Cloud backup

If you are signed in, you can upload one cloud backup. That backup is a copy of the business profile, clients, invoices, quotes, and settings on the phone at that moment. A later upload replaces the previous copy. It is not a live sync.

The copy is stored in Firebase so that only that signed-in account can read or replace it. Restore puts that copy back on the phone and replaces the records already there. You are asked to confirm before a restore.

Delete your account and data

  1. Open InvoiceFlow, open Settings, open Account, and choose Delete account. Confirm it. This removes the account and its cloud backup. Invoices, clients, and settings already on the phone stay on the phone.
  2. Delete individual clients, invoices, or quotes in the app. This removes those records from the phone.
  3. Delete the InvoiceFlow app. This removes the records kept only on the phone.

If you cannot open the app, email developer@aqyrix.com from the account email with the subject “InvoiceFlow account deletion”. Aqyrix will delete the account and its cloud backup.

Payments are handled by Apple or Google. Deleting the account or the app does not cancel a charge they already made. InvoiceFlow Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Sharing a document

Export, print, and share send a PDF or a backup file only to the app, file, or person you choose. InvoiceFlow does not upload that file except when you use cloud backup.

Crash reports

InvoiceFlow uses Firebase Crashlytics to find crashes. A crash report can include the error, the app version, and the device model. It is used to fix the app. It is not used for advertising. It does not include your invoices.

Notifications

If you turn notifications on, Firebase Cloud Messaging creates a device token so a reminder can be delivered. You can turn notifications off in InvoiceFlow settings or in the phone settings.

Purchases

The app can be downloaded without paying. The free version includes up to 5 clients and up to 10 invoices per month. InvoiceFlow Pro is bought through Apple or Google. Apple or Google processes the payment. InvoiceFlow does not receive your card number. The store tells the app that Pro was purchased so it can be restored.

What InvoiceFlow does not do

InvoiceFlow does not sell your information. The app does not show ads and does not include a third-party advertising tracker.

Children

InvoiceFlow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to developer@aqyrix.com.
