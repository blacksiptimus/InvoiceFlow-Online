InvoiceFlow support

Help for the InvoiceFlow app on iPhone and Android. Published by Aqyrix Technologies.

If something in InvoiceFlow did not work, or you have a question about an invoice, a backup, a purchase, or your account, write to developer@aqyrix.com. A person at Aqyrix reads that address.

What to include

A short note is enough. These details make the answer faster:

  1. iPhone or Android, and the app version from Settings, About.
  2. What you were doing, such as creating an invoice, exporting a PDF, restoring a backup, buying Pro, or signing in.
  3. What you expected, and what happened instead.

Do not send a password. Do not send a client list or an invoice file unless we ask for it.

Account

You can delete an account in the app: Settings, Account, Delete account. If you cannot open the app, use the steps on the privacy policy.

Contact

Email developer@aqyrix.com. The privacy policy explains what the app does with information.
